# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We expect contributors, maintainers, and users to treat one another with respect.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and factual.
- Assume good faith until the evidence says otherwise.
- Respect differing experience levels and perspectives.
- Keep feedback focused on the work, not the person.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Deliberate trolling or repeated disruption.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Any conduct that makes collaboration unsafe or hostile.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, block participation, or restrict access when behavior violates this code of conduct.

## Reporting

If you need to report a concern, open a private issue or contact the maintainers through the project’s usual communication channel.
